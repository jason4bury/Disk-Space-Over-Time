Help

Disk Space Over Time reads how full each of your drives is once a day and keeps every reading, so you can see a drive filling up long before it runs out. It only reads sizes: it never opens, changes or deletes a file.

Reading the page

  • Each drive has a card with its free space now, a bar for how full it is, and a chart of the space used over time. The dashed line across the top is the size of the drive.
  • Last 30 days is how much more (or less) space is used than 30 days ago.
  • Full in about is when the drive will be full if it keeps filling at the rate of the last 90 days. The dashed line on the right of the chart carries that rate on. A drive needs a week of readings before this is worked out, and one that has barely changed shows as Not filling up.
  • A card is marked Low on space when under 10% is free, and Filling up when it will be full within 90 days.
  • Hover over (or tap) a chart to see the day's reading. Month, 3 months, Year and All at the top pick how far back the charts go.
  • Readings under a chart lists them as a table, and Download CSV gives every reading of every drive as a spreadsheet.

When readings are made

Every day at 03:00, and the first time the app runs. If the computer was off or asleep then, the day's reading is made as soon as it's back. A drive that can't be read (an unplugged drive or a sleeping NAS) is tried again every 30 minutes that day; nothing is recorded for it until it answers, so a gap never looks like an empty drive. Record now reads every drive at once and replaces today's reading.

Choosing the drives to check

Open Settings: only the drives under Drives to check are read. Add a drive on this machine lists the drives mounted into the app in docker-compose.yml (if any). Add a drive over SSH reads a NAS or another computer: press Make a key under SSH key, add the line it shows to ~/.ssh/authorized_keys for that user on the other machine (the line only lets the key run df, which lists drive sizes), then fill in the address and press Show its drives. Rename and Remove change the list; a removed drive's readings are kept, and come back if you add it again.
